# Modern constellation-line data

The constellation line patterns in `constellations-modern.json` are derived from the
[Stellarium Modern sky culture](https://github.com/Stellarium/stellarium/tree/master/skycultures/modern).

Stellarium attributes the sky-culture text and data to the Stellarium team under the
[Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
The generated dataset is distributed under the same CC BY-SA 4.0 terms.

The line figures are cultural and illustrative conventions, not official International Astronomical
Union boundaries and not physical relationships between stars. The IAU formally defines
constellations as bounded regions of the celestial sphere and does not prescribe a single
stick-figure pattern.
