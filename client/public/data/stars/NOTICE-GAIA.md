# Gaia DR3 data notice

The files under `gaia-dr3-tiles/` are calculated aggregates of 2,923,790 records selected from the
ESA Gaia Data Release 3 `gaiadr3.gaia_source_lite` table.

- Credit: ESA/Gaia/DPAC
- Release DOI: <https://doi.org/10.5270/esa-qa4lep3>
- Release overview: <https://www.cosmos.esa.int/web/gaia/data-release-3>
- Data model: <https://gea.esac.esa.int/archive/documentation/GDR3/Gaia_archive/chap_datamodel/sec_dm_main_source_catalogue/ssec_dm_gaia_source.html>
- Query, retrieval date, source hashes, transformations, and scientific limits:
  `client/data-sources/gaia-dr3/README.md`

The generated positions, magnitudes, and colours are calculated cell summaries. Their rendered
point size, opacity, and colour mapping are illustrative. They are not individual searchable Gaia
sources and do not claim volume completeness.
